# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring teams, collaborators, and freelance clients visit this portfolio to understand Warit Panyeam's fullstack capability quickly. They are scanning for credibility, technical fit, project range, and a direct way to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

The site introduces Warit as a fullstack developer who can build polished interfaces and reliable backend logic. Success means a visitor can understand his role, scan selected work and skills, and reach the contact section without friction.

## Brand Personality

Clear, capable, precise. The voice should feel confident and practical, with enough visual presence to make the portfolio memorable without turning it into a generic developer template.

## Anti-references

Avoid heavy SaaS landing page cliches, identical icon-card grids, decorative glass panels, oversized metrics, vague buzzwords, and developer shorthand that relies only on monospace type or terminal styling. Avoid copying the reference site verbatim.

## Design Principles

Lead with role and name before decoration.

Make the first viewport feel like a complete portfolio identity while hinting at the next section.

Keep technical skill scannable for busy hiring readers.

Use interaction only where it clarifies navigation or lets visitors inspect work.

Keep contact actions visible, direct, and low effort.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve keyboard focus states, semantic section structure, reduced motion behavior, and responsive layouts that avoid horizontal overflow on mobile.
